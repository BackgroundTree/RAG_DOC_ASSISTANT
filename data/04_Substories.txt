Substories
A long-standing tradition in the Yakuza series are substories. These are mini-dramas that you can engage in in order to help the populace of Japan in their lives. These substories are almost all entirely optional, and you'll have to seek them out in order to get involved. In addition to giving you fights to build yourself up, many substories will also lead to items that you could not otherwise get normally. Still others tie into Minigames or your Business.

There are 100 substories in this game, but don't be off-put by the size of that number. Many other distractions in this game are also put into the substory menu, such as making friends with people in town and getting involved in training. Those tend to be really quick substories that just set up something else for later.

Also, while you can mess up on substories in that you won't necessarily get the best items from them, you cannot "miss" substories in terms of completion or part of the story. All substories can be accessed at the very end of the game right up until you engage the final boss, so don't feel like you necessarily have to get them all done as they appear.

Some games in the series have offered markers noting where substories are. While that's unavailable at the outset of this game, there is an accessory called the "Trouble Finder" that will put exclamation marks "!" in spots where they can be found. Also, once you begin a substory, if it has a second part that occurs elsewhere, you can find a "!" mark on your map to know where that spot is, even if you don't have the Trouble Finder.

Substories are ordered in this guide by the chapter they become available. This may go without saying, but only the character available in that chapter can complete substories that become available in that chapter.

Lastly, sharp-eyed viewers may notice that there's a problem with the numbering of the substories. Some numbers are missing, while others appear twice. That seems to be a problem that existed even in the first version of the game and they didn't bother fixing it for the localization for whatever reason. At any rate, I've given these the same numbers that are provided in the game.

Overview
Given the fact that this game breaks a tradition of marking substories on the map, I feel I should provide an easily referable overview on how the substories are broken apart, so that you, the player, know when to expect them easily, particularly if you're not in the mood to scroll through a big list of substories spoiling how to beat them.

Normal - These substories are typical of the series. You'll come across someone on the street who needs help, and you can help them to solve the story. It may involve moving to new areas to continue or complete the story.
Business - These are more or less normal substories, except they're intricately tied to your business. This is more obvious in the Cabaret Club Czar set but there are also a few that are specific to Real Estate Royale.
Minigame - Substories tied to a particular minigame: Pocket Circuit, Telephone Club, or Disco. Usually these go through a progression of several substories.
Friendship - These substories tend to go very quickly to completion, but after you complete it, you'll get a secondary goal of striking up a friendship with the person involved, measured by a bar that fills up. You can typically improve this friendship through partaking in their business or something similar. Maxing out the friendship will get you something extra, but it's not required for the sake of substory completion.
Training - All Masters have their own substory, which is quite simply beginning their training. Once you start the training, the substory completes.
Shakedown - Between the two characters, there are four members of the Mr. Shakedown tribe. Beating each of these guys once starts and completes their substory.

Chapter 2 Substories
All of these substories will only become available after Kiryu returns to his apartment for the night as part of the story. They open up the next morning, as he's looking for info on Tachibana Real Estate.

#2 - Arakure Quest
Outside the Don Quijote is a conspicuous line of people. Speak to the young lad at the end and he'll let you know it's a line-up for a hot new video game and he'll be getting the last copy available. What a trooper. Leave the area and come back and you'll see the kid being accosted by a no-goodnik who will take his game. Run down the alley towards Pink St. and you'll see that guy get counter-accosted. Now, hurry up Pink St. towards Taihei Blvd. and... I think we're getting the pattern here. Beat this guy down (mind his knife) and then head west along Taihei to find the yakuza guy near the save point. Ruin him (mind his GUN?) and you'll finally get the game back for the sprout. Your reward is a Sunburst katana.

#4 - Kamurocho Undercover
Go to Shellac in the Champion District and you'll see a guy in a newsboy cap sitting in the first seat. Try to sit at the further seat and you'll be shooed away. Once the scene plays out you'll learn about a gun trade operating out of the bar. Once you leave, walk away for a bit and then a marker will appear over Shellac on the map. Return to the bar and speak once again to the reporter. You'll head outside and beat up the gun dealer. Your reward for this is a 9mm Automatic Pistol.

#6 - Damned Yanki
To begin this, head south from Children's Park towards Mach Bowl. An odd old man/punk will run across your path, just as you cross Shichifuku St. Head further into Theater Square and you'll see some people gathered, and that same man off to the side. Speak to him and he'll ask you to teach his group how to really get in with the "yanki" style popular in Japan around this time. What you get as a result of these depends on how you answer. There are two good answers and one bad answer for every question. The good answers are: "Start your engines!" or "Hello in English", "Fighting every day" or "Rescuing puppies", and "The time you crushed a gang" or "Stay silent". If you get good answers for all three, you're rewarded with a Wild Shirt. If you only get two, you get a Charismatic Autobiography. If you only get one, you get the Charismatic Photo. Krazy Kyo will also become an advisor for your business once you get access.

#7 - How To Train Your Dominatrix
For some odd reason, you have to complete Substory #9 first to make this appear. Right near the Heroine Karaoke Bar, you'll see an obvious woman in an S&M getup. Approach her to hear her speaking to a custommer and then speak to the woman afterwards. Looks like Ayu needs some help in her line of work. First, deal with the jerks harassing her, then you'll head to the park (in view of children, because whatever) and the two of you will go through a role-play, with Kiryu as the submissive. You'll have to pick lines for Ayu to say. I've recently learned that there are a couple of ways to go about this. Here are the options for the responses:

This first option is for being a successful dominatrix. First is "Who gave you permission to speak, pig!?". For the second you can pick whatever you like. For the third, "I bet you'd like that, you pervert!". Now, the kids show up, so use them and select "They're watching you get humiliated!". Ayu will get her groove back and you'll get a Fur Belly Warmer.
Alternatively, you can choose to steer Ayu towards being more of a "tsundere" type. Select "It's not like I'm happy to see you or anything!" Secondly, again, it seems anything works. Third, "Gross! I'm the only who would do that for you!" When the kids arrive, "Keep your eyes on me, and me alone!" You'll create a whole new brand of dominatrix and will get a Collapsible Steel Staff as a reward.
If you waffle in your direction, which is to say, if you choose a lame answer, a good answer, and a tsundere answer, you'll get a Toughness Z, but everything will largely wrap up the same way. The "Ayu Sakurai" video will be viewable at Gandhara. Also, once you get access to your Real Estate business, "Masochistic Man" will be hanging outside your office to join as an advisor.

#8 - Password Protected
In the Kamuro Shopping Area, go to the south end of the wider street to see two guys. One gives the other a password but Kiryu didn't pick up all of it. Talk to the merchant and he'll mention that you need the password. Head out the north end of the Shopping Area and speak to the guy who was just shopping there. He'll send you to find a woman named Monmon, who will be marked on your map on the east side of Taihei. Walk to up to her and say "Plans for today" and "Tomorrow's weather" and YOU FIGHT NOW. Beat down the four dudes (careful of their weapons) and Monmon will give you the password: "a movie date" and "rain at noon". Head back to the guy at the north end of the Shopping Area and give him the news. He'll go make a deal and give you a Broken M1985. Head back to the dealer. You'll actually have to type in the password, so tell him "Subotenuhigeukiro", and yes you have to capitalize the "S". This gives you access to this dealer as a shop with weapons and gear. Also, once you open the real estate business, Li Leung will want to join as security.

#9 - The Show Must Go On
Near the Shrine are a couple of guys chatting. Approach them and they'll mention their need for a producer. Or really, they just need someone to stand there and look like a producer. Agree to their proposition and you'll get a fancy costume change. During the course of the shoot, you'll be asked to do a few things. First, you'll have to give your greeting, and anything works fine. For the next part, you'll have to grab an "erasable". That's the food (because it can be erased from the scene by being eaten). Next, you'll be asked to "grocer" the food. Your response should be to "tilt it at an angle". Lastly, you'll be asked to "jeer" and "strike the set". That means to "remove the dish". If you get it all right, you'll receive a Magnetic Necklace. Get too many things wrong and you'll only get a Tauriner +. After all that, you'll have to fight the real producer and his cronies.

#12 - Miracle on Tenkaichi Street
You must first complete "The Show Must Go On". Head to the small lot near the western Akaushimaru south of Theater Square and you'll hear two guys talking about the American pop star, "Miracle Johnson". Sounds like one smooth criminal. Leave the area and come back once a marker appears on the map in that area and you'll find Papillon Kato, the director from before, who's shooting a "thrilling" music video. Anyway, agree to his request and, long story short, you'll have to protect Miracle from approaching hordes of zombies. I suggest using Beast Style as the zombies will swarm and their whole schtick is knocking you down, so it's best to grab a big weapon and use Beast to demolish them. As a reward, Miracle will offer you the choice of a black or white box. Choose black to get Silent Shoes, and choose white to get a Baseball Shirt. I suggest black because a Baseball Shirt can be bought very easily in town. Furthermore, once you start the real estate business, both Papillon Kato and Spining will want to join your business as managers and will be found waiting outside.

#17 - Underneath It All
Head east along Shichifuku St. starting from Maharaja and you'll come across a high school couple arguing. Talk to the young man afterwards and he'll mention that his girlfriend might be selling herself. Tragic. Head down to Senryo Ave., where the marker is, and you'll find Mina there. Speak to her. The dialogue choices you make are largely unimportant, but they get to the heart of the matter that Mina is involved in "burusera", where Japanese high school girls sell their used clothes to whoever is willing to buy. She'll mention Sachiko up by the Hotel District as being the ringleader, so you'll be looking for a long-haired girl wearing a school uniform with a white collar and a blue scarf, and she might be wearing a cardigan. Head up that way and you'll find a veritable gaggle of girls. Sachiko is the one near the plants on the west side of the street, with the blue scarf. Confront her. You'll have to choose all the options, but Sachiko will just tell you off. Walk away and a problem will show up. Deal with him and you'll close out the substory. Once you open the real estate business, Sachiko will want to join as a manager.

#24 - The Girl in the Video
After viewing three videos at Gandhara in Kamurocho, return and you'll see another customer. I won't spoil how this goes, because frankly... I don't want to, but agree to the guy's proposition. Anyway, looks like you're looking for a girl. I'll just tell you that she's hanging out at Kanrai. Once you spot her, head back to the front of Gandhara and let the man know. Go back to Kanrai and then you'll fight alongside him against a couple of ne'er-do-wells. Once you open your business, Video Boy will want to be security.

#28 - Don't Dance Alone
Dance three times at the Maharaja and you'll be approached by a goofy-looking fellow. He'll challenge you to a dance battle. Essentially you'll have to play Friday Night and beat him, and difficulty doesn't matter. You'll get as many chances as you need to win.

#29 - Technique to Die For
You must complete "Don't Dance Alone". Leave the Maharaja, return, and go to the bar to be approached by Maiko. She'll challenge you to a dance battle. Beat her at Queen of Passion. She's better than the first guy, so be wary.

#30 - Maharaja Showdown
You must complete "Technique to Die For", leave and return to Maharaja. You'll now see Ogita on the dance floor. This is your final dance battle challenge, and he's a tough customer. You'll have to shoot for extra steps and hit Fever at least twice. I suggest dancing on Normal instead of Easy, because he'll make more mistakes on Normal. Once you win, compliment Ogita and you'll get an Isobe Fan. Bye, Ogita! See you in Yakuza 5!

#36 - I am Kazuma-kun
After winning a couple of races in Pocket Circuit, you'll be introduced to Takuma-kun and Hideki-kun. Walk towards the shop booth in the Stadium and Takuma-kun will approach you. Accept his challenge to race. Defeat him to close the substory and get a Blue Bolt decal.

#37 - The Rocky Road of Romance
After completing "I am Kazuma-kun", head to Children's Park to find Takuma-kun and Hideki-kun. Hideki-kun's new friend shows up and she's a GIRL! Oh, the drama! Head back to the Stadium and find Hideki-kun, then wipe the floor with him on the Beginners' Cup.

#38 - Gift of Love
After completing "The Rocky Road of Romance", speak to Mika-chan and she'll challenge you for her "most important thing". This is where these substories start to get difficult. Defeat her using your Rookie's Race build and you'll receive a DRAG-ON car and a set of Boost Gears. I'd like to point out that after this point, being able to complete the next set of substories requires a bankroll in order to buy the necessary parts, so don't be afraid to leave this chain for a while until you can come back with much more money.

#39 - Suspicion of Perversion
After completing "Gift of Love", leave the stadium, come back, then head towards the Parts Shop. Your next challenge is against Satoru-kun. Use your Pro-Am build to beat him.

#40 - The Prodigious Racer
After completing "Suspicion of Perversion", it's time to meet the first of the Three Elites. First up is Sena-chan the Ace. Defeat her with your Experts' Race build. You'll get a Sunshine decal for the win.

#41 - The Woman They Call Professor
After completing "The Prodigious Racer", head to Earth Angel to challenge the next of the Elites: Harumi-chan the Professor. Defeat her with your Experts' Race build and you'll get a Cool Striker car and a Heavy Suspension.

#42 - The Greatest Glory
After completing "The Woman They Call Professor", it's finally time to defeat the last of the Elites: Kazuyoshi-kukn the Champion. Use your King of Speed Cup build against him to win. You'll get a Dark Purple decal for the victory.

#43 - The Fighter's Crown
After completing "The Greatest Glory" and completing all the races, Pocket Circuit Fighter will finally challenge you himself. Your setup is a special one and is listed above in the chart. The first two laps are where this is decided, because you will start to fly off as you reach the back, so be ready to tap the button but don't go overboard, because you'll likely have to hit it again on the jump. Do the same on the second lap. The third and fourth should be fine comparatively. Once you win, everyone will come by to congratulate you. You now have the ability to talk to anyone in the Stadium and challenge them specifically.

#46 - The Stop-and-Search Specialist
South of Gandhara Kamurocho, you'll see a cop standing around. Approach him and he'll ask to see your belongings. Pick something innocuous (like food or an energy drink) and he'll become your friend. To cultivate the friendship, continue to return to where he's standing. He'll do a "stop-and-search" in order to see if you're carrying something dangerous. Show him something normal to raise friendship. Showing him something dangerous (like a weapon) doesn't have any negative effects, but your Friendship won't raise. At certain times, you'll hear his story. Once the meter gets high enough, you'll have to fight off some bad guys harassing a nearby lady. You'll get a Sacrifice Stone as a reward, as well as the Nanase Otoha video clip. Lastly, once your business opens, Kikuchi will want to join as security.

#47 - The Mushroom Merchant
In the northeast part of town is Park Alley, which is just southwest of the taxi stand. At the end of it is a rotund fellow looking to sell mushrooms. After the conversation, you'll fight a dude. As a reward for saving him, you get an Enokitake and Kitajima as your friend. Buy his mushrooms to raise friendship. Once you buy enough to continue his little story, he'll thank you and leave the area. Leave yourself, then return to the same area later and you'll find some more uglies harassing him. Beat them up, then buy more mushrooms from him until he walks off to stock up. Leave and come back again, and now there are three guys to beat up. Offer him a new suggestion (try whichever you like), buy more mushrooms, then leave and come back once more. Now you have to beat up four guys from overseas and that will close out the Friendship quest. Once you open up the real estate business, the Mafia Boss will want to become security for you.

#50 - Pocket Circuit Fighter!
Head to the Pocket Circuit Stadium on the west side of Shichifuku St., enter and speak to Pocket Circuit Fighter, then speak to him again and learn the tutorial to Pocket Circuit. This will complete the substory and you'll get PCF as your friend. To increase friendship with him, beat successive races and you'll learn his story after each race. This also ties in with the Pocket Circuit series of substories as well. After beating the Rookies' Race, PCF will run off. You'll see him on the east side of Nakamichi St. Speak to him and tell him "The kids are waiting for you" to get him back to the Stadium. Once you beat the Champions' Cup, he'll run off again. Go find him in Public Park 3. You'll get the Devil Killer car as a reward for maxing out the Friendship. Also, you'll be able to make your own custom tracks to race on.

#51 - Miho, Convenience Store Clerk
Buy something at the Poppo on Tenkaichi St. and Miho will introduce herself and you'll get her as a friend. Keep buying things from her and at 50% friendship, she'll ask what might help attract people to the store. Offer a suggestion, then leave and come back. If she uses your suggestion, you'll get a Berserker Charm. Also the correct answer to her next question is "Miso Cutlets" and you'll get a Miso Cutlet Lunch Set, as well as Miho's video. This will also allow you to get the property for free once you start your real estate business.

#52 - Emiri, Mach Bowl Receptionist
I'm not sure exactly what's required to get Emiri's attention. It may just be that you'll have to bowl in general, but it's also possible that you may be required to get a few strikes in order to spark her interest. Once you finish a round and she's impressed enough, she'll introduce herself. Continue to bowl to increase friendship steadily. Once you hit 80% on the friendship meter, she'll mention a special event, but you won't be able to participate until you start Real Estate Royale in Chapter 5 and defeat the Leisure King. Speak to Emiri at that point and she'll challenge you to bowl a turkey: three strikes in a row. Do so and you'll get... a chicken. And Emiri's video. The chicken will be named Nugget and instated as a manager. Cluck.

#53 - Sushi Gin's Chef
Eat a couple of times at Sushi Gin and the chef will become your friend. Continue eating to raise the Friendship Gauge and continue conversations with him. He'll ask if you can bring him fish. Head to the Pier via taxi to find the fishing minigame and start reeling them in. He'll need about four fish total for you to bring him, and crazily enough, it can be any kind of fish. Give them to him one at a time. After the fourth, leave and come back and you'll get a cutscene to finish the friendship. This will allow you to get the property for free once you start your real estate business.

#54 - Luka at SEGA HI-TECH LAND
Play Out Run at the arcade and Luka will take notice. Play again and she'll mention working on her high score. Come back in and she'll bring up her score of 1.5 million. Beat that and you get an Alertness Hood. Play once more and then she'll comment again. Come back and she'll give you a score of 3 million to beat. Do so and Super Hang-On will be installed in the arcade. You'll also get Luka's video. This will also allow you to get the property for free once you start your real estate business.

#89 - Bacchus' Training
This will only pop up late in Chapter 2, after you give alcohol to the homeless guys. Bacchus will call you into the nearby alley, so go there and help him take down his debt collector.

#90 - Kamoji's Training
This is a quick substory to set up Kamoji as a Master. Go to the Shichifuku Parking Lot and speak to him.

#91 - Miss Tatsu's Training
This is a quick substory to set up Miss Tatsu as a Master. Go to the Pier in Tokyo and speak with her.

#92 - Apex Predator
Run into Mr. Shakedown (after your first meeting). This man in a trilby is the one of two in Kamurocho. His name is Egashira. Use your best skills to bring him down, or just cheat and spam Heat Actions with medicine to bring yourself back up.

#93 - A Dream Unfulfilled
The second Mr. Shakedown is a guy in a purple jacket named Sato. Beat the tar out of him to close out the substory.

Chapter 3 Substories
The substories don't pop up in this chapter until you finish your encounter with Fei Hu and get the Slugger Style.

#49 - From the Heart
Just outside of Le Marche is a man sniffling over not being able to buy something. Approach him and he'll tell you to find him a heart necklace. Or, failing that, a convincing forgery. Head down towards Shofukucho South and you'll run across two guys talking, one obviously being the forger you're looking for. He'll give you his business card, so pass it off to the man in front of Le Marche. You'll hand off the business card. Now, head down towards Shofukucho South again to the marked spot (where you found the forger earlier) and defeat the three guys inside. You'll get a Secret Wallet as a reward and the Riri Kuribayashi video clip.

#50 - The Entertainer's Throne
On the east side of Iwao Bridge is a fellow playing at a human statue. Examine him to speak to him. Leave and come back later to find a crowd watching him, but it appears he has to use the restroom. You'll need to distract the crowd. The correct options are the "UFO" and "singing" options, but you can try all for funsies. After that, you'll fight a tough guy, which should also be quite distracting. Now, speak to Statue-san again. Successfully getting him to the restroom will net you a Staminan Royale. If you don't do so hot, you'll get a Staminan X.

#51 - Rise of the Dragon
Head to the far east of Shofukucho St. near the taxi and someone will try to get your attention. At the north side of the street is a guy hiding behind a pillar. Speak to him and you'll learn of his predicament. You'll learn of the "bontan hunter" who hangs out near Magutako. Head over there and you'll find a VERY familiar looking blond kid (if you've played other games in the series). He's a tough customer with a lot of health and power, so don't be afraid to get tough with him. Afterwards you'll have a nice conversation with "Ryuji-kun" and his friends. Return to Peter Pantsless and give them back. Head back to Magutako to close out the substory.

#52 - Be My Boyfriend
Head down Sotenbori St. and around the time you hit Zuboraya, a woman will stop you and ask for you to be her boyfriend. Respond however you wish, then agree to her request to meet her dad as her pretend boyfriend. Answer as follows: "Pleased to meet you sir. I'm Goro Majima." "I'm a dentist." "We sat next to each other at a movie." "She's so innocent and childlike." Your max reward is a Sprite Belt. for getting everything right. Finale Substories
These become available after the final fight with Kuze. If you're familiar with this series at all, I'm sure you know what's coming...

#94 - The Head of the Clan
Once you complete all of Kiryu's substories but one, your pager will make some odd noises and tell you to come to the bullfighting arena. Take a taxi to the arena and you'll face Kiryu's bonus boss: So Amon.

Prepare for this fight. Fill up your inventory with Incomparable Habu Drinks and get the best Mail armor you've made from Dragon & Tiger. May want to get some Sacrifice Stones on you as well. Don't worry about weapons.

We start right in Looney Tunes land as So whips out a cannon. He has three attacks:

Fires straight at you, which you can dodge by running to the side. Any cannonballs have a chance to either stun you or wreathe you in flames, putting you on the ground.
Release a volley of shots above him. Some of these fall randomly, others are shot towards you. Try to be unpredictable in your movements so the minimum hits you. If you get hit, you get hit, just stand back up and start the fight again.
If you're distant from So or stunned, he'll fire a black and red laser. This is EXTREMELY damaging and if you get caught flat-footed with this, you'll likely croak. It does have a cooldown though, so take the moment to charge him.
For this phase of the battle, I suggest using Legend Style. The trick is to use the "Finishing Hold" move, which is hitting Grab after a Finishing Blow (Heavy Attack at the end of a Rush Combo). Any Finishing Hold will put So on the ground. After that, press Grab near his head to pick him back up, which leaves him in a stunned state, and you can hit him with another Finishing Hold, and then continue like this.

Once you wear down three of his health bars, he'll pop Heat. This will give him super armor and he won't flinch to any of your hits. It's still the best policy to use the Finishing Hold into Grab near his head on the ground. You may get unlucky and get tagged once he falls out of stun. Try to Grab him and throw him to the ground if you have to start over.

Once six health bars are gone, he'll switch to unarmed combat, in a style that matches Kiryu's final boss from the story. He'll also call in a helicopter that drops debris on the battlefield. The really hard part is avoiding all this villainous patio furniture, because it'll bounce around with the wind from the chopper.

Now the trick to this half of the fight is not necessarily to watch Amon or the furniture, but look for small black and gold bottles dropped on the ground. These are invincibility potions, and whoever gets it is invulnerable for a short while, represented by flashing white. If Amon gets it, back down and run off until it wears off. If you get it, now's the time to wade in and ignore the distractions and just beat the heck out of him. How you do it is up to you, but I personally enjoyed using Beast Style on the nearby furniture. It's good damage.

That's the remainder of this fight, in a nutshell. Bottles will drop throughout the fight, so keep on top of them to deny them to Amon. Once you beat him, you'll get Amon Sunglasses.

#100 - Sotenbori in Peril
Once you complete all of Majima's substories but one, go to Sotenbori and your pager will make some odd noises and tell you to come to the bullfighting arena. Well, that's not what Majima thinks it says, but he ends up putting together anyway. Take a Taxi to the arena from Sotenbori and you'll face Majima's bonus boss: Jo Amon.

Prepare for this fight. Fill up your inventory with Incomparable Habu Drinks and get the best Mail armor you can make from Dragon & Tiger. May want to get some Sacrifice Stones on you as well. Don't worry about weapons. Don't worry about specific abilities, because we're going Mad Dog Style for this one (which you must have in order to get to this anyway).

Jo Amon has thirteen health bars and is wise to almost all your dumb tricks. There is one ace up your sleeve that you'll use against him: Mad Dog Trick: Demonfire. That's the move where you hold the Fighting Stance button, then press Heavy Attack just as your enemy attacks. It cuts deep into Amon and is pretty much the only way you're guaranteed to cause damage.

Amon begins the fight using Kiryu's Legend Style. He'll also throw stuff at you. You'd think those are knives, but check your inventory. If you had any empty space, he threw tissues and "Stanamin Spork" in there. Don't use the Spork, because it will drain your health. Once you drain about eight of his health bars, he'll pull out a knife and basically work his own Mad Dog magic on you. Keep pressing the Demonfire attack over and over. WATCH your health, because if he gets hits on you with his knife, your health will drain HARD, so be ready to jump into the Pause Menu and drink up. The same strategy works, though. Once you defeat him, you'll be rewarded with Amon Sunglasses.
