Real Estate Royale
Kiryu's Business involves taking on the Five Billionaires of Kamurocho and bringing justice to the world of real estate by beating them at their own game: buying up properties and then channeling those funds into buying more and more until you reign supreme as the big Monopoly king.

This major side story is begun in Chapter 5. After your initial encounter with the property owner Yamanoi, he'll take you under his wing and declare you president of his real estate business. You will be walked through a series of tutorials which include making collections, buying new properties, and utilizing your staff. Following that, you'll be basically left to your own devices, and can buy and improve however you wish.

The very basic idea of this business is to buy properties, spend money to improve properties, collect from properties to make money, then funnel that money back into spending more. It's essentially working a series of menus and then waiting a few minutes for the money to come rolling in.
__________________________________________________________________________
Basics of Real Estate
Access your business from the model of Kamurocho in the Sugita Building on Suppon St. From there, you'll get a menu that will allow you to view the Properties you own, the Staff you currently employ, and you can also review the help files you were given at the outset. Those selections are just for review, though, and the real action is going on in the "Kamurocho Real Estate Royale" section. From there you can select a district.

Making Money -
The first and most important function on this menu is that you can press Triangle (Y on Xbox, X on Switch 2) with any district selected (you only have one to begin with) and you'll start Collections in that district.

Collections will take a particular amount of in-game unpaused time to complete. For example, the first district starts at ten minutes and if you go to the Shrine and purchase upgrades with your Completion Points, you'll be able to reduce that wait, which is very recommended.

You can review how long the Collection needs to complete either at the model in your office or on your Pause Menu under Business. You'll see a bar that fills in over the course of the time.

Once collection is finished, return to the office's model and press the Confirm button on the district and you'll get the profits with a plus or minus due to economic trends. Then you can start Collections again, and repeat the process as many times as you want to. It's basically free money with the only thing spent being your time.

Making More Money -
As the game walks you through your first collection, I'll skip ahead to what happens when you press the Confirm button on a district. You'll have two tabs here: Stores and Staff. Press the shoulder buttons to tab between the two.

Stores gives you a menu that lets you see the properties you own in that district as a list and each property's individual ranking. From here you can apply advisors to increase the ranking of those properties. Higher ranks means the property gives more money in Collections.
Staff shows you which of your Managers and Security you've assigned to that district. Make sure you assign a person to each and make sure you select them so they appear in the slots in the bottom left of the screen. If you do not assign staff, you run the risk of losing properties or dealing with Trouble Events.
Much More Money -
So, that's the menu taken care of. The other aspect of Real Estate Royale is actually hitting the pavement and buying new properties. The game will direct you towards your first acquisition in Sukiyaki Muranaka, but every other property in the game you'll have to find on your own by searching, and very few of them are obvious to someone who doesn't understand Japanese. Fortunately, I have a list later on in this section you can use to zero in on where properties can be found. Approach the property and you'll get a prompt called "Look" and you'll see a small window that will tell you how much you need to purchase the property. They usually start in the fifteen million range and go up as you get to newer districts. Once you buy the property, you are free to improve it and it will automatically be added to that district's collection, even if you've already started a collection run.

Why All This Money? -
Your goal in each district is to obtain at least a 90% share in it and to defeat the "King" that runs the district. You can increase your share by:

Buying properties
Improving rank of individual properties
Winning Money Battles
Participating in events against the Five Billionaires. Your share can also decrease if you lose these events, so be aware of it and save often so you don't lose too much.
Once you defeat the King of that district, then you can continue to collect money on it without having to worry about security problems or Money Battles.

Once you've done this for all five districts, then Real Estate Royale is essentially "complete" and you can just use it as a money spigot to drown yourself in.

Your Staff
You are not alone in your efforts to buy out the town. You'll have a staff on hand which you can utilize to improve your chances of making massive amounts of cash.

Managers are available in order to affect your income and participate in Money Battles.
Security are used in order to protect districts against ne'er-do-wells.
Advisors are used to improve individual properties to their maximum earning potential.
You will start your business with two of each of these staff members, but they'll be low-level proles. To get the most out of your staff, you'll have to go out and cultivate some relationships, and those come in the form of substories.

If you do your due diligence and get involved in substories from early in the game, you may already find that when you start your business you'll have people literally lined up outside the Sugita Building looking to help you as part of your staff. Speak to all these people to get them on the team, and be sure to continue to get involved in substories, as you'll continue to pull in more people.

Additionally, completing districts will also give you new staff, in particular the "Kings" of each area and often their #1 lackey.

Below are a list of all staff members, their function in detail, and how you can get each of them.

Managers -
The function of managers is twofold. They help prevent your properties from being taken over in Money Battles, and they apply a modifier to the income you get when you collect from that district. You can assign a manager to any district as long as you're not already collecting.

Managers have two attributes. They have "Financial Savvy", tracked as one to five stars. This determines how much "HP" they have for Money Battles, which we'll cover in a moment. They also have "Economic Influence", meaning how they positively or negatively affect the economic trends in the area, which affects your profits.

This influence, rather than being a single number, changes over the course of how many collections you perform with them. The first number is the modifier on the first collection run. The second is on the next collection run, and so on. For example, the first shift you stick Tsuneo Hirata on a district, he'll have an influence of "0". If you leave him there for the next shift, it will change to "1". For the third, it will stay "1", the fourth will drop to "0", and the fifth will drop to "-1".

A positive number is better, and as you'll notice, everyone "ends" on a negative number, meaning that for the fifth and subsequent collections, you'll be stuck on that negative number, meaning you probably should switch them out for a different and less tired manager. Fortunately, the actual effect is relatively negligible. Yes, it's a percentage, but once the big money starts rolling in, you won't be missing that extra two or three million.

Security -
Security also serve a multiple function. Their primary function is to deter "problems" from showing up in your district that stop the collection process that Kiryu would have to solve on his own. Their secondary function is to reduce the number of random encounters in that district while collection is taking place. A neat little tertiary function is that if you get into a random battle in the district they're watching, there's a chance they'll join in on the fight and assist you.

The attributes of security folks are "Problem Solving", which is a one to five star rating. The higher the rating, the less chance you have of a "Collection Problem". The other attribute is "Safety Influence". This is a number like the manager's Economic Influence, but all this affects is the enemy encounter rate in your district, so all you're worrying about there is its effect on you personally. To that end, security isn't something that has as high priority as managers or advisors, so don't feel like you need top-notch security at all times.

In fact, once you defeat a Billionaire of a particular area, you don't have to assign a Security person to that area at all anymore.

Advisors -
The function of an advisor is to improve a specific property. All properties have a letter grade denoting how good they are at pulling in income. D rank is the lowest. S rank is the highest. When you select a property in your menu, you have the choice of supplying an advisor to that property for a fee. That property will be improved based on how effective that advisor is with that particular type of property. Once you use the advisor, a marker reading "Invested" will be put on that property that will persist until the next collection. You cannot assign an advisor to that property again until you initiate a collection run. That said, there's nothing stopping you from improving multiple properties in a single collection time. Furthermore, you can apply these improvements at any time, even while collection is happening.

There are two general rules as to how much an advisor will improve a property. The first is ranking, again measured by one to five stars. Quite simply, a higher ranking will improve more at a time. The second rule is the type of properties that they're proficient in. You'll see a little chart with each advisor with symbols denoting their proficiency.

The first row are the types of properties you can buy:

Fork and Knife - Restaurant (R)
Shopping Cart - Shopping (S)
Star - Leisure entertainment (E)
Heart - Health clubs or sexy entertainment (H)
Building - Offices (O)
Beneath each of these are a second symbol:

X - Not proficient at all
Triangle (Δ) - Minor proficiency
Circle (O) - Moderate proficiency
Double Circle (◎) - Major proficiency
So the rule when using advisors is to use the best proficiency with the property in question, unless it's cost prohibitive. Essentially, early on you'll be using the cheaper folks, then once you get into the Gambling King's stuff and beyond, just go ahead and use Miracle Johnson for most properties considering he's the top option and you'll have the cash by then.

Challenges of Running a Business
Every business comes with risk, and your risks are in the form of your opposition: the Five Billionaires. They have two means by which they can disrupt your business: Money Battles and Collection Problems.

Money Battles are initiated by the King of the area and start as you come to pick up your collection. Your first one will probably surprise you out of nowhere. In short, mash the Confirm button until the battle stops. What the battle is doing is basically pitting your manager against the King as they try to wrestle control of a property you own. As the King "attacks" your manager's HP will start to drop. Mash the button during battles to throw some of your own money (100,000 per tap) and replenish that HP. If the HP empties, the King gets back one of your properties and you have to go buy it again. If you win, then you'll keep all your properties. If you don't have a manager currently assigned to that area, then the King will win automatically and take back a property. HP reduction for a manager persists, so if you want managers to replenish, switch them out in favor of a different one.

Collection Problems will halt a collection as it's ongoing. You'll see notice of that in the Business section of your menu and on the map you'll see a red X in a circle. Personally walk to that location on the map and you'll see a few dudes spoiling for a fight. You'll also get a forecast as to how difficult the fight is expected to be. Defeat the dudes and collection will resume. Having a good security person will reduce the probability of these events happening.

The Five Billionaires
Every so often, your quest for monopoly will result in the attention of the Five-Bills. Depending on whose territory you're invading, you'll get thrust into fights and competitions with the Kings.

You can only face the Leisure King at first. After his defeat, you'll be able to address the areas of the Electronics King, Pleasure King, and Gambling King. After all four of them are down, you can take on the Media King.

The Leisure King
30% - Your very first encounter with the Leisure King comes even before you start this, so it's not like I need to point it out. Your second encounter, however, entails him getting up in your face as you leave the Sugita Building. He'll dispatch his toadies after you, so defeat them all.
60% - Next, once you return to the office, Marina will let you know that you've been challenged to appear at the karaoke bar. Head there and perform better than 80 points in Judgment -Shinpan- to get more of a share of the LK's area.
90% - Lastly, once you have a full 90% share of the neighborhood, the Leisure King will call you out for a showdown. Head to the back of Serena and you'll have it out. The Leisure King is actually a rather tough fighter for this point in the game, and he has Takeshita who is no slouch, plus two other nerds. Watch for LK's stunning punch.
His defeat unlocks the Brawler Style's fifth and sixth ranks. You also get Leisure King as a manager and Takeshita as security.

The Electronics King
30% - Nothing special happens. Marina just describes the Electronics King to you.
60% - You'll get challenged to go to the arcade on Nakamichi. Head there and beat up his goons, then beat the Electronics King's score on Out Run. It's five million, which incidentally is the completion amount, and that's NOT easy. Drive quickly, but also carefully.
90% - In the office, you'll get a call from the King. Head down to the Nakamichi St. arcade and you'll fight the King and his minions. EK has a swift style. I suggest countering it with Beast Style. There are tons of things on the ground in this area to use against him, so let 'er rip.
His defeat unlocks the Rush Style's fifth and sixth ranks on the left side of the grid. You also get Electronics King as a manager and Namiya Meiden as security.

The Pleasure King
30% - Info on the Pleasure King.
60% - Go to the office and you'll get called out to an adult shop. Head to Senryo Ave. and go north along it until you spot the Pleasure King hanging outside the night club "Heaven Gate". Approach and you'll be challenged to a "teleclub battle". Basically you have to get on the Telephone Club and get a date within three minutes. If you've been practicing, it's not hard at all. Afterwards, you'll fight a trio of lackeys.
90% - Return to the office and then you'll head back to the Heaven Gate night club again. You'll fight the Pleasure King and those three lackeys again. I suggest going Beast Mode on them, slamming the bicycles and signs in the area all over. The Pleasure King is a grappler strong type, so either stay out of range with Rush or match strength for strength with Beast.
The Pleasure King's defeat unlocks the Beast Style's fifth and sixth ranks on the right side of the grid. You also get Pleasure King as an advisor and Jaguar as security.

The Gambling King
30% - Info on the Gambling King, as well as a notice that you can't actually meet him until you get access to the Japanese Catfight Club in Kamurocho, part way into Chapter 6.
60% - Once you've unlocked the JCC, return to the office and you'll receive your challenge from the Gambling King. Head to the JCC and you'll face him in a single match.
90% - Head to just outside Mach Bowl and you'll square off against the Grumble King and his four nerds. It's actually not that difficult a fight. Smash him hard.
Defeating him unlocks the remainder of the Rush Style. You also get the Gambling King as a manager. Also, you can finally access the Media King's District, if you did this one last of the middle three.

The Media King
30% - Info on the Media King.
60% - Marina has been kidnapped and taken to Maharaja. Head there and have a dance battle against the Media King. He's as good as Ogita or Miss Isobe, so be ready to bring your A-game.
90% - Head to Shichifuku Parking Lot to confront him. You'll fight him and five of his goons. The goons are all pretty tough and the MK himself is a very speedy fighter with a lot of health. Don't be afraid to cheat by bringing a bunch of Heat and health replenishing items.
Following this, you'll be introduced to the Finance King, the one pulling all the strings behind the Five Billionaires. Defeat his two lackeys, then you'll unlock the remaining blocks on Beast Style.

Now, head back to the office and you'll learn of the true role of the Finance King. Head to the entrance of Tenkaichi Street and talk to the Pleasure King to be taken to the Billionaire's Room. Take out those two guys from earlier again, only they're stronger now. After that, you'll fight the Finance King, and there are plenty of broken pieces of his table to use with Beast Mode. That flash you just felt at the end was Kiryu realizing the DRAGON OF DOJIMA STYLE, his Legend Style. This closes out the Real Estate Royale story, although you'll likely still have some work to do to get all the completion metrics.
